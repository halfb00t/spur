Read every `ci` run since the `ubuntu-latest` rollout to Ubuntu 26.04 began (2026-10-19) and classify the first one that
runs on 26.04 from its whole logs against Phase 26's pre-classification (REQ-ubuntu26-classified-on-migration, SC1-SC3).
The phase is date-gated, so the plan has four outcomes, and none of them may fabricate a reading:

- `green`: the read and the debt item's retirement land in one commit, and nothing goes to the human (26-02's precedent).
- `red`: the read is committed with `ci.yml` untouched. The human picks `pin` (`runs-on: ubuntu-24.04` plus L46) or `hold`.
- `not fired` (no job of an `ubuntu-latest` run in the window on 26.04; a pinned-label run like 26-02's spike is listed but
  not counted): the dated read is committed. The human picks `wait` or `close`. `wait` leaves the plan without a SUMMARY
  so a later `/gsd-execute-phase 29` reads again. `close` is the ROADMAP's close condition.
- `pending` (an `ubuntu-latest` run on 26.04 exists, but there is no reading yet of all three jobs on 26.04): the dated
  read is committed, and the human's only choice is `wait`.

Task 1 is the tracer, the whole reading path in one go: gh listing, per-job logs, the record and the outcome's commit.
Task 2 is the human's call. Task 3 carries it out.

Purpose: the debt item's procedure, applied to the real migrated run, never to the calendar.
Output: the Phase 29 section of `bench/RESULTS.md`, the raw window lines and logs, and the outcome's ledger edits.
