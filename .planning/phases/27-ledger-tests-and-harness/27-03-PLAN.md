## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| test harness -> OS process groups | the teardown sends SIGTERM/SIGKILL to a process group it spawned |
| `ps` output -> teardown verdict | an external reader decides how long to wait and what to report |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-27-10 | Denial of Service | an orphaned uvicorn group surviving a failed `ps` (T-21-02) | medium | mitigate | SIGTERM and SIGKILL are sent before and regardless of the read; a failed read is `survivors unknown`, a failure; proved by the failed-ps unit test |
