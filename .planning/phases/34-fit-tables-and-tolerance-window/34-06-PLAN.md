<objective>
Make the pin-contact guard live at the standard's own form-circle limit and let an odd tooth count print where the
standard tabulates it. For a listed size the window's lower bound becomes the copy's d_Ff1 maximum plus the margin (D-12),
which retires the internal construction that sat above that maximum on most rows (the must debt item); each of the three
measurements goes through it on its own. An odd tooth count prints M_Re and its limits at the tabulated pin, where 211 of
211 odd Part 2 rows agree (D-11), and stays null off the tables.

Purpose: the guard protects the numbers that now come from the copy, at the copy's limit, and an odd-count shaft becomes
checkable where the standard says how.
Output: three signature changes in shaft_calc.py, ~8 tests, one debt item resolved, one idea retired.
</objective>
