## Flagged Assumptions

- **A1 (the 1e-6 mm comparison).** No decision states the precision contract for the new wall rules.
  The existing rules compare raw floats and their tests avoid exact boundaries; the edge probe asks for
  the exact boundary, and at it the step-aligned sets compute 5e-16 mm under MIN_WALL. Rounding the wall
  to 1e-6 mm before comparing accepts a wall the user sized to exactly MIN_WALL and still refuses anything
  a micron thinner. It is used by the new rules only (surgical; the old rules keep their behaviour).
- **A2 (the hole_circle_d and hub_d bounds).** CONTEXT recommends le 400 from `recess_inner_d`'s family.
  On gears with a root diameter above about 400 mm (200 teeth x module 10 is 1975 mm) holes stay within
  the inner 400 mm, the limit `recess_inner_d` already has. Raising an `le` later is additive (L05-safe).
