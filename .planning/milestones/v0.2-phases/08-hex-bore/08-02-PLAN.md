Primary noun: the bore's outer extent, `calc.bore_rim_limit(p)` (the farthest point of the bore wall) and its chamfered form `calc.bore_mouth_limit(p)`.
Decision: **promote**. `recess_radii()` switches its datum from `bore_radius(p)` to `bore_rim_limit(p)` and `bore_mouth_limit(p)`, so every consumer of "how far the bore reaches" (recess hub, selector band, the 08-03 root rules) reads the general extent. `bore_radius(p)` is demoted to a detail of the round variant: it cuts the circle and feeds `bore_effective` only. Add-alongside (keeping `bore_radius` as the recess datum and bolting a hex case on beside it) was rejected: the recess would clear the wrong radius for any hex wider than the round bore.
