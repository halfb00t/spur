## Flagged Assumptions

- **A1 (a third cap term, D-04's mechanism).** 10-01 measured the kernel failing inside D-01's and D-02's caps wherever the root fillet's straight lead-in reaches above the pitch circle (a large profile shift or root fillet for the module). The failure point is exactly ra - spline_start on every configuration bisected. D-04 pre-decided the response: the cap is lowered to the measured boundary, it is tested one step either side, and it is never a 422. It is not the "no single rule" case that D-04 routes to a checkpoint: one analytic rule held on all 169 configurations measured. It is conservative by up to 0.44 mm on 17 of 163 grid sets, where the kernel would build more. The rule's form, a geometric radius the outline already computes rather than a measured constant, is the planner's reading of "the cap becomes the measured boundary"; it is recorded in L29 (10-05). Reversibility: costly, flagged and not gated, as D-01 and D-02 are.
- **A2 (the selector checks the end faces).** RESEARCH recommended a radius-only selector. The planning probe found that after the chamfer, 2 x teeth arcs at radius ra remain at z = c and face_width - c, so the selector also requires both endpoints on an end face. That is CONTEXT's discretion option of "`_groove_floor_edges`'s shape ... start point on an end face". It makes "at both end faces" true by construction and lets 10-03 prove that no tip arc remains on an end face after the cut.
- **A3 (the warning prints the applied value with :g).** The sibling warnings print `:.2f`. Here `:.2f` would print 2.94 for a 2.937 mm cap, a number past the kernel's contact and different from tip_chamfer_effective. `:g` of the rounded value prints what /api/info prints (L08).
- **A4 (one new `type: ignore`).** `solid.chamfer` on a `cq.Shape` needs `attr-defined`, exactly like `_cut_bore`'s call. The count goes from 4 to 5. 10-05 updates `docs/tech_debt/active/2026-09-21-cadquery-shape-typing.md`, whose title says four.
