  the default reference_d 25 and 18 teeth needs a shift of +15.45 and is refused by the range check. The m 0.5 cap is

- D-05's "(tip_d - root_d)/2, which is exactly module" holds on typed decimal inputs (13,552 rows, 32-01-floor-grid.log);
  for arbitrary floats each diameter is rounded to 0.001 on its own, so the printed tooth height is within 0.0005 of the
  module and a multiple of 0.0005. The cap is computed from the printed diameters, the radii the solid uses (32-RESEARCH
  OQ1), so `tip_d/2 - c - root_d/2` stays a multiple of 0.0005 for every lattice c.
- The cap warning fires only when a limit binds at the 0.001 mm resolution (`floor_mm(request) > applied`), the gear's
  10-REVIEW CR-01 rule (calc.py:1122-1131): a request of 1.0004 cut at 1.0 is the print resolution, not a cap.
- Final warnings order: `(FORM_DIAMETER_UNPROVEN, *pin_warnings, hobbed root, *measure_warnings, *reading.warnings,
  ROOT_FORM_CONSTRUCTION, *chamfer warnings, SHANK_STEP_NO_RELIEF when shank_length > 0)`: appended, so no Phase 31
  sentence moves.
