<objective>
Prove the basic dimensions and the shift against an oracle that shares no code with them (REQ-shaft-basic-dims,
REQ-profile-shift-from-dB; ROADMAP Phase 31 criterion 2, "agrees with an independent stdlib oracle ... seen red once
against a mutated formula"; D-08 vendor rows; Claude's Discretion oracle layout after tests/trochoid_oracle.py).

The oracle rebuilds the shaft from textbook definitions, not from din5480's closed forms: d_B from the basic rack's
addendum plus the tip clearance, the tip and root from the rack, and the nominal tooth thickness by locating the point of
a parametric involute on the circle m*z, the involute placed by the base-thickness identity. A wrong constant or sign in
din5480 then disagrees with it instead of moving both sides together (L08). The two root formulas are the same
definition written twice, so their comparison proves the transcription only; the comment says so. 31-03 extends the same
file with the pins, the contact and the form diameter.

The copy's anchor rows are not here: no number from the standard exists in the repo before 31-05 (D-06). The vendor
rows give the comparisons real numbers in the meantime and never count toward the copy rows (D-08).
</objective>
