<objective>
Print the pin diameter and the measurement over pins at nominal thickness, keep the form diameter and odd-z M_Re honest
(None with a reason), and build the pin-contact guard as a function of an explicit pin (REQ-mre-nominal,
REQ-form-diameter-honest; ROADMAP Phase 31 criterion 3; D-10, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-20, D-22).

Planner choices recorded here:
- Gauge step (D-11): 0.01 mm. Source: D-11's default; 31-RESEARCH A4 found no documented series for a theoretical pin
  (the vendors' effective pins are round values, 6.000 and 5.500; the only gauge-series pages found were paywalled). The
  31-05 checkpoint asks whether the copy names one; changing the step then is a decision, not an edit.
- Guard margin (D-12): `GUARD_MARGIN_FACTOR = 0.1`, times m, on both ends. D-12's 0.030 mm disagreement (Hexagon's printed
  113.91 max against the public construction's 113.880) is 0.01*m at m 3; KISSsoft's two values for one shaft (63.920 by
  DIN 5480-1 Table 5 against 63.718 constructed) are 0.202 mm, 0.067*m. 1.5 times the larger gives 0.1*m, so the margin
  covers both observed gaps; scaling with m is an assumption (both gaps are at m 3). Over 7,392 cases the theoretical
  pin's contact sits at least 0.62*m above the internal d_Ff and 0.886*m below d_a (D-20), so a wrong margin costs nothing
  until Phase 34.
- The internal d_Ff is the public construction (the rack's flank-to-tip-arc point, tip radius RHO_FP*m, generated onto
  the shaft): sqrt(d_b^2 + (d*sin(a) - 2*(h_fP*m - x*m - rho_fP*m*(1 - sin(a)))/sin(a))^2). Checked at plan time:
  113.8802 for W120x3x38 at h_fP 0.55 and 63.7180 for W70x3x22 at h_fP 0.60. Valid where that rack point generates above
  the base circle, which holds over z 6 to 82 and x -0.05 to +0.45 for h_fP up to 0.65 (plan-time check).
- `measure_over_pins` returns None both when the inversion target is outside (0, inv(89 deg)) and when the tangent point
  would lie at or below the base circle (tan(a_M) <= pin_d/d_b): a pin touching off the involute is not a flank
  measurement (31-RESEARCH Pitfall 5b).
- D-10's note is appended by derive_shaft for every shaft: through derive_shaft the pin always prints (D-20 measured),
  so the note is never attached to a null pin. Phase 34 replaces both the pin and the note.
</objective>
