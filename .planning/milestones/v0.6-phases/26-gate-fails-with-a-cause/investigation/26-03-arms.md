## Reading

- The real app agrees with the stand-in on all three arms, 3 of 3 each. The decision gate (Task 1 step 4) did not
  trip, so no `checkpoint:decision` was returned.
- SC2's wording ("a delayed STL route makes the old step red") does not hold on the real app: arm 1, the strongest
  form of a delayed STL route (parked, never released), leaves the old step green, because `app.js` issues the STL
  request only after the `/api/info` response (`update()`, `app.js:266-274`), and the old baseline is taken after the
  info request, before that response is acted on. The lever that moves the old baseline is the `/api/info` response:
  arm 2 holds it, takes the old baseline, releases it, and the STL request then lands inside the counted window (one
  extra request, `assert 279 == 278`). Arm 3 takes the baseline only after the page's own `/api/model.stl` request
  has been seen and is green under the same parked `/api/info`.
- The 350 ms debounce does not bear on the baseline at load (`update()` runs directly at load, `app.js:643-644`, so
  no timer is pending); it bears on the zero-request assertion, which the CX-05 control (Task 2) measures.
